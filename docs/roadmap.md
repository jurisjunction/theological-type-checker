# TTC-ROADMAP(7)

## NAME

**ttc-roadmap** — the steps done, the steps to come, and the theories
waiting to run

## DESCRIPTION

The foundation is built in the order of the *Summa* and of what each step
needs. Each step's predictions are committed and stamped before it is
encoded (rule 12), then scored.

## AMENDMENTS

To be committed and stamped before the step is encoded.

- **TTC-CHRIST: presence typed by mode.** A body is in a place
  circumscriptively, an angel definitively, God by filling it (ST I
  q. 52 a. 2). The deposit adds two: sacramental presence, "in many other
  places" while Christ sits at the Father's right hand "according to the
  natural mode of existing" (Trent, sess. XIII, ch. 1, DH 1636); and the
  glorious body, "not limited by space and time but able to be present how
  and when he wills" (CCC 645).
- **The kernel's world.** What counts as one creature becomes input. The
  unity of an organism is a school's; for the human being it is defined
  (Vienne, DH 902).

## CANDIDATE THEORIES

To be run on the system, as the *Grammar of the Icon* was. None enters the
kernel.

- **The postman.** Spacetime a creature through which bodies act on one
  another; spirits speak without it, "local distance does not impede"
  (ST I q. 107 a. 4).
- **Branching history.** The past never undone; a new branch made
  instead (ST I q. 25 aa. 4–6). Constraints the deposit sets: the cross on
  the trunk, "once for all" (Heb 7:27; 10:10); one return and one judgment
  (Heb 9:27; DH 1000); no fabricated pasts (DH 3008). The kernel holds an
  order without cycles, not a line, and no theory of time
  (`causes_precede` is a part).
- **The Passion as the root of creation** (Behr). Christ first in
  intention; "human" typed by end, not by nature, so that the image stays
  with everyone (Ignatius, *Romans* 6.2; Irenaeus).
- **Interpretations of quantum mechanics as runtimes**, tested against one
  soul as the form of one body and one judgment (DH 902, 1000).
