# TTC-FINDINGS(7)

## NAME

**ttc-findings** — what running the checker found, mostly wrong with itself

## DESCRIPTION

Each entry is machine-checked; test IDs are in parentheses. None is a
verdict on a disputed question. Each reports the structure of a dispute,
not its winner.

### 1. Univocity of mode costs something (S3, S4, UN1–UN3)

Give the grain of origination one mode on both sides of the analogy, and one
of two things happens:

- **Necessity everywhere.** An existing child of an impeded union is
  reported as impossible (UN1).
- **Norm everywhere.** God is put under a norm (UN2).

A single license for "two as one principle" either breaks the Filioque or
puts a nuptial circuit inside the Trinity (S3, S4). Both assume the Filioque;
without it they vanish, and UN1 stands alone (§13). The analogical modes give
a norm breach in creatures and nothing impossible (UN3).

The checker's bridge (one form, two modes, God first) is compatible with
Scotus's univocity of *concept* with intrinsic modes (*Ordinatio* I d.3 p.1
qq.1–3; I d.8 p.1 q.3). What fails is univocity of *mode*. The polemic that
runs the two together (Milbank, Pickstock) is answered on this point by
Cross (2001) and Horan (2014). **ttc** cannot distinguish analogy from
Scotus. It can distinguish both from univocity of mode.

### 2. Weight is not prediction (B1–B5)

In the Immaculate Conception backtest, the weight of Bernard, Bonaventure
and Aquinas ran against Scotus from 1400 to 1661. *Grave nimis* (1483) and
Trent (1546, DH 1516) protected the question, so the minority school stayed
open. Alexander VII tipped the preference in 1661. *Ineffabilis Deus* (1854)
deleted "no". Without the protective acts, "yes" would have survived only
as a shadow after 1661 (B4).

### 3. Two axioms overstated their modality (B7, L1–L4)

- **P3** said "cannot" for "ought not". Read descriptively, it is falsified
  by every child conceived outside a valid marriage.
- **A9** said "is" for "is called to be". It silently excluded limbo, a
  school *Auctorem fidei* protects (DH 2626). Reading A9 as MD 25 words it
  fixed this and changed nothing else.

Every axiom now needs a modal type.

### 3a. A prohibition stated too broadly (X5, X5b)

X5 forbade ranking states of life "by magnitude". Trent teaches that it is
"better and more blessed" to remain in virginity (Sess. XXIV can. 10, DH
1810); FC 16 calls the charism superior "by reason of the wholly singular
link which it has with the Kingdom". As worded, the prohibition contradicted
a tier-1 canon: refutation condition 1, narrowly avoided. The tradition
supplies the distinction. The gift of self is total in each state (FC 16).
The excellence of virginity is its link with the Kingdom, not a larger gift.
X5 now forbids only the measuring. X5b holds Trent's canon beside it,
settled, and nothing is violated. This was found while writing the
exposition in the discipline's own terms, not by the checker.

### 4. The trinitarian images fail in opposite ways (AN9–AN11, T6)

- **The family** keeps the persons and loses the processions.
- **The mind** keeps the processions and loses the persons.
- **A complete map** violates Lateran IV.

The two images are complementary, not rival (*De Trinitate* XII; MD 7).

### 5. Tier 2's force is an assumption (B9–B14)

LG 25 is interpretive on the point (Gasser's relatio). The CDF's 1998
commentary is tier 3, and *Ad tuendam fidem* is legislation. Either one,
read as fixing tier 2, is self-elevation.

### 6. Form, not author; governance is not teaching (B12–B15, LT5, LT8)

A papal act's tier follows its form. The Code binds to teaching by
reference and teaches nothing itself.

### 7. Recency points opposite ways (DR2, DR3, DR3b)

Remarriage turns on one typing. Read as governance, the later act governs.
Read as teaching, repetition across popes counts and recency does not.

### 8. Communion is a partial order (EC1–EC10)

*Dominus Iesus* 17 ranks communities that the bond list (UR 3) leaves
incomparable. Membership is binary and communion is graded; *extra
ecclesiam* is consistent on that typing and Feeneyite on the other.

### 9. Tier 3 is not flat (G1–G4, CU, SA, DR3b)

The four kinds of internal structure are legislator rank, document
character, custom and salience. Adding them changed one earlier result.

### 10. SSPX, 1988–2026 (SX1–SX23)

[cases/sspx-2026.md](cases/sspx-2026.md) has the detail. In brief:

- **Incurred, presumed, declared and suspended** are four states.
- **"Process superfluous" is c. 1717 §1.** It dispenses the investigation,
  never the hearing of c. 1720 n. 1.
- **On the record, the 2016 grant stands** until a competent revocation.
- **The note's "invalid"** holds for some acts and not others.

### 11. Canon law is binary (correction)

The first SSPX encoding put juridical questions on the authority graph,
where canonists weighed against dicastery notes. A canonist objected. The
objection was correct (cc. 16, 18, 19, 135 §2), and the canon layer
replaced that encoding. One result reversed.

### 12. The ordered pair (PR1–PR4)

The pair is (teaching, law). Coupling the layers made it explicit that the
school denying the 2026 act was schismatic needs c. 751 read as a state.
Uncoupled, the law applies c. 1364 where its premise fails (PR2).

### 13. The Filioque is independent of the grammar (FQ1–FQ20)

Formally undecidable, in Gödel's sense: neither it nor its denial follows
from the axioms.

State M1 with the Spirit from the Father alone (Photius, *Mystagogia*;
Gregory of Cyprus, *Tomus*, 1285; Palamas, *Capita* 150, 36). No axiom
fails (FQ1). Of the other 149 scenarios, 13 change; the rest are identical,
and a test pins the set:

- **The Trinity** (M1, M5, AN1, AN6, S4): the Spirit's principle is the
  Father. No person is both from another and principle of another.
- **Receiver-as-lesser** (X1): still a violation, one instance fewer.
- **One license** (S3, AN3, UN2): the costs shown in God vanish. Nothing in
  God is from two, so the divine license is idle (FQ3).
- **The images** (T6c, AN9–AN11): the family loses even its shape;
  Augustine's mind keeps one procession.

Every creaturely, canonical and authority result is unchanged.

What decides the question is a premise outside the axioms (FQ4):

| License | Distinction of persons | Filioque | Monopatrism |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| one power (Lyons II) | by mode too (Nazianzen, Damascene) | consistent | consistent |
| one power | by opposition only (Anselm; DH 1330) | consistent | Son and Spirit indistinct (ST I q.36 a.2) |
| none (Photius) | by mode too | two principles (S3) | consistent |
| none | by opposition only | two principles | Son and Spirit indistinct |

Each deciding premise took its classical form inside the controversy:
Anselm's axiom in *De processione Spiritus Sancti* (after Bari, 1098),
the two causes in Photius's *Mystagogia*. Whether either has a warrant from
another case is the next question. The Latin side argues opposition from
simplicity (ST I q.28 a.3). §14 encodes it: it follows on Thomas's reading
of simplicity, not on Scotus's.

Both doctrines keep the monarchy: the Father alone is principle without
principle (FQ1, FQ2; DH 1331). PCPCU (1995) says the same, keeps
*ekporeusis* "from the Father alone", and refers the Filioque to
*processio*.

Each psychological image fits its author's doctrine and no other (FQ11–FQ15).
Augustine's mind (love from mind and word) licenses both processions only
under the Filioque. Palamas's (love from the mind, resting in the word,
*Capita* 36–37) licenses both only under monopatrism. Images confirm; they
do not decide. Nazianzen's Adam, Eve and Seth (Or. 31.11) has the
Filioque's whole shape and licenses nothing (FQ16, FQ17).

Palamas's eternal manifestation through the Son is a sixth relation type.
A0 admits it with a warrant from another case (the essence–energies
distinction, argued on the light of Tabor) and refuses it without one (FQ7,
FQ8). Mediation is not origin: the Son still holds no second position.

In the authority layer, Lyons II (DH 850) and Florence (DH 1300–1302)
settle the question at tier 1 (FQ18). Remove them and it is open, both
branches clean (FQ19), unless distinction by opposition is added (FQ20).

The grammar does not decide the Filioque. The councils do.

### 14. Classical metaphysics of God (TH0–TH20c)

Add the metaphysics of God from a list fixed by others: the attributes as
defined (DH 800, 804, 3001–3002, 3025), then the 24 Theses (DH
3601–3624), then Thomas's readings. Rerun everything at each step.
Predictions were committed first;
[ttc-thomism(7)](research/thomism-preregistration.md) scores them.

The whole stack goes in. No default scenario turns red.

What conflicts, and why:

- **Receiving in one mode** (TH2): potency in the Son and the Spirit, each
  really distinct from the essence, the quaternity Lateran IV excludes
  (DH 804). Thomas keeps receiving analogical (q.42 a.4 ad 1).
- **Necessity carried to creatures** (TH16): the modal-collapse inference
  makes every creature necessary, against DH 3025. Mixed relations (q.13
  a.7; q.19 a.3) remove it.
- **Bridges read in the order of knowing** (TH8): every bridge becomes a
  projection. q.13 a.6 reads them in the order of being.
- **Procession denied its analogy** (TH20, AN4; not predicted): Thomist
  simplicity then distinguishes nothing, and the persons are one. The
  Cappadocian properties (unbegotten, begotten, proceeding) still
  distinguish three. Thomas founds the relations on the processions (q.28
  a.4), and states the processions by analogy (q.27).

Each is a fault in a bridge: one mode on both sides, the wrong direction,
or none.

Between schools:

- **Simplicity decides the Filioque only as Thomas reads it** (TH12): only
  opposed relations distinguish in God, so the Son and Spirit need one.
  This is the warrant from another case that §13 asked for. Scotus held
  the Filioque and denied the inference (*Ord.* I d.11 q.2; TH14). The
  decisive premise is a reading of a defined attribute, not the
  definition.
- **Thomist simplicity refuses** Scotus's formal distinction (TH13) and
  Palamas's energies (TH15b). The definition refuses neither (TH15c).
  Thomas's distinction of reason sits between the synonym reading and
  Scotus (TH13b).
- **Clarke** passes immutability as defined and fails q.13 a.7 (TH17,
  TH17b). Process theism fails both (TH17d). The core passes both (TH17c).
- **Clarke and Long on receptivity** (TH18): the grammar is neutral. The
  treatise's sentence needs Clarke's bridge, God first.
- **Th. 5 refutes Suárez** in its own words (TH3); the core is neutral
  (TH3b).

Against the treatise (TH5): §2 called the creature's relation of creation
"not an accident added to a creature already constituted". ST I q.45 a.3
ad 3 says the creature is prior to it "as the subject is to the accident".
The distinction of transcendental and predicamental relation reconciles
them; the treatise now says so.

Already in the core: eternity and infinity (crossing guards), Th. 15
(A1), Th. 24's first two clauses (A1). Th. 7–14 and 16–20 share no
vocabulary with it. The core had no bridge for goodness (TH19c).

Two of three meta-predictions failed. Both failures are in the list
above.

### 15. The kernel's own world (K1–K14)

Give the kernel creatures of its own, across the range of created being
(ST I q.18 a.3; q.76 a.3; Lateran IV, DH 800): stones, stars, an oak, a
bacterium, lions, humans from embryo to adult, a sculptor and his bronze,
two angels. Then the positions it must refuse: God the cause of himself,
a theogony, a demiurge, a loop of ancestors, Arius, a bridge with no
divine mode.

The kernel refuses each (K3–K9). It also had two errors of its own:

- **Created person was read as human.** The image (GS 12) and equal
  dignity (GS 29) were stated over all created persons. With angels
  present, a ranking of natures ("a little less than the angels", Ps
  8:5) became a violation of equal dignity. Both now range over human
  persons (K10). On whether angels bear the image, the deposit is silent
  and Thomas affirms it (ST I q.93 a.3): a school's question (K12).
- **Two givers of being were a norm breach.** Only God creates (ST I
  q.45 a.5; Th. 24), so a second giver is impossible, not wrong. A3 now
  types it so (K5).

And two errors of the theory, fixed by its author's decision:

- **The norm of origin reached lions** (K1b). A3 made one principle of
  origin a norm for creatures. A lion cub has two parents and no bond. A
  norm binds rational agents; A3's norm now binds persons only.
- **The call to be bridal reached angels** (K1c). A9 ranged over created
  persons; MD 25 speaks of "every human being". A9 now ranges over human
  persons.

"Giving", taken as a property, does not tell God from a stone (K2): T1's
vacuity holds across the Creator–creature line, not only among persons.

The whole Thomistic stack fits the world. Only Arius gains a violation
(potency in the Son made a creature) and univocity is refused (Th. 4).

### 16. The schools as runtimes (RT1–RT14)

Four schools packaged as runtimes, each a named list of parts loaded whole:
Thomist, Scotist, Suárezian, Palamite, over a shared classical base
(*agere sequitur esse*). The four school statements left in the kernel,
and the deposit model's line for one power, moved into a default runtime;
no scenario changed. Predictions were stamped first
([ttc-schools(7)](research/schools-preregistration.md)): 21 of 25 held.

- **The deposit asks what only a school answers.** With no runtime, the
  defined Filioque gives the Spirit two principles (RT1). Lyons II
  defines one principle (DH 850), not how. The Latin runtimes answer by
  one spirative power; the Palamite runtime needs no answer.
- **Each school boots.** The Thomist runtime changes seven scenarios,
  the Suárezian two, the Scotist none. Scotus's univocity of concept, with
  distinct modes, raises nothing (RT5): the kernel types modes.
- **Every clash between two schools lands on one of three instructions:**
  how simplicity is read, what distinguishes the persons, and Th. 5 on
  essence and existence. Univocity clashes only if Th. 4 is read as a rule
  on predication (RT6, RT6b). *De auxiliis* never clashes; two readings
  stay two branches (RT12c).
- **One leak.** A runtime can remove a fact of the deposit's model: the
  Palamite runtime's monopatrism retracts the Filioque of M1 instead of
  being refused (RT11). The refusal comes from the authority layer
  (FQ18), by the rule of the ordered pair.
- **Florence's axiom of opposition (DH 1330) two ways.** Read as deposit,
  the Filioque follows inside the grammar; the "mode" rows of the
  Filioque table collapse into the "opposition" rows (RT14); nine
  scenarios turn red, among them Scotus's counterfactual (RT13) and
  Nazianzen's properties when the analogy of procession is withheld
  (TH20b). Read as Thomist, nothing changes. The checker prices each
  reading; it does not choose.
- **The Grammar of the Icon is Latin.** Not predicted. Its analogy of one
  principle takes its divine side from one spirative power. On the
  Palamite runtime, or with none, every scenario of the theory is refused
  (RT3, RT9).
- **"Giving" is vacuous because creatures act.** §15's result, that a
  unary *donative* does not tell God from a stone (K2), holds only under
  *agere sequitur esse*. With no runtime, stones do not act, and the claim
  comes true (RT2).

### 17. The human act as a library (AC1–AC11)

Canon law presupposes a theory of the human act it never states. Thomas
treats the act (ST I-II qq. 1, 6–21) before law, "a rule and measure of
acts" (q. 90 a. 1). The theory is now one library (`act.lp`) that the
canons call; none keeps an interior slot of its own. Predictions were
stamped first ([ttc-act(7)](research/act-preregistration.md)).

- **The interior is input.** What a case claims of the agent's knowledge,
  will or freedom is `interior/3`, supplied or computed both ways when
  disputed; no rule derives it, and a test says so. Routing c. 1321 §4
  and c. 1323 through the library changed no existing scenario.
- **One fear, four canons** (AC8, AC9). A grave fear unjustly inflicted
  from without, typed once: the juridic act stands but may be rescinded
  (c. 125 §2); the marriage is invalid (c. 1103, where the fear need not be
  unjust or intended, but must come from without); no penalty
  (c. 1323 n. 4); a tempered one where the act is intrinsically evil or
  harms souls (c. 1324 §1 n. 5), and then no latae sententiae penalty
  binds (§3).
- **Dolus and culpa are compositions** (AC6, AC6b): the violation known
  and willed; the omission of due diligence. c. 1321 §3 is one switch per
  norm.
- **Canon law presupposes that creatures act** (AC10, AC11). On the bare
  runtime no human act exists, and no penalty is incurred, the SSPX case
  included.

### 18. Law as a library (LW1–LW9)

Thomas defines law before any code (ST I-II q. 90 a. 4), orders the kinds
of law (q. 91), and says when a human law fails (q. 96 a. 4). The Code's
general norms state the same theory as rules of the external forum. The
theory is now one library (`law.lp`), and cc. 7–8, 14, 16–21, 85–86 and
135 §2 call it. Predictions were stamped first
([ttc-law(7)](research/law-preregistration.md)).

- **The reach of an authentic interpretation** (LW1, LW2). *Ecclesia Dei
  adflicta* reads c. 751: a single act of disobedience "constitutes a
  schismatic act". Given in the form of law (c. 16 §2), it binds every
  case. Given as an act in a particular matter (c. 16 §3), it reaches the
  1988 consecrations only. Its form is disputed, and the checker computes
  both. In the second branch c. 751 has no authentic interpretation for
  2026, c. 18 reads it strictly for the penal effect, and the 2026 title of
  c. 1364 for Bp Fellay is not derived; c. 1387 still reaches him as
  co-consecrator. Approved by the Pope, the 2026 decree would itself be the
  legislator's act in that matter, and the title would return.
- **The suite had assumed more than the ordered pair says.** Three
  scenarios (SX11, PR1, PR2) asserted that c. 1364 applies wherever the act
  is classified as schismatic. The pair requires only that the law not
  apply without its theological premise. Penal law, read strictly, may
  decline a title the classification would allow. The expectations now
  hold for each form of the letter.
- **The mind of the legislator, by rank** (LW3). A reading on which the
  supreme legislator's law would contradict the natural law is excluded:
  his law is read in harmony with it. A bishop's law keeps the reading, and
  on it does not bind (c. 135 §2; q. 96 a. 4). Both rules are binary.
- **Doubt of law, doubt of fact** (LW4). In a doubt of law an invalidating
  or obliging law reaches only what every reading reaches (c. 14). A doubt
  of fact leaves the obligation and lets the ordinary dispense.
- **Equity is input** (LW6). An equitable rule (c. 19) is supplied, or
  computed both ways when disputed, never derived; in a penal matter it
  cannot supply a penalty (c. 221 §3).

### 19. The deposit as the creeds profess it (CR, CS, CM, CE)

The kernel held the deposit only where the *Grammar of the Icon* needed
it. It now holds the Incarnation, Mary and the economy as the creeds tell
them, with Scripture above the creeds as tier 0. Predictions were stamped
first ([ttc-creed(7)](research/creed-preregistration.md)).

- **The kernel never said who Christ is.** The incarnate was a person of
  human nature, not divine, and the theory's models named him `christ`,
  apart from the Son. With the union encoded, the seven scenarios that
  load the Trinity with those models found a fourth divine person (CR2b).
  The models now name the Son.
- **Everything said of him is said by a nature.** Typed by the person
  alone, his birth of Mary gives the Son a second principle of origin
  (CR4b), and his growth and passion are change in God, on the Thomist
  runtime potency in pure act (CR5b). Typed by nature, nothing fires: the
  two births of Constantinople II, one principle.
- **The Father was born of Mary, and the kernel allowed it.** Not
  predicted. T5 typed a term by its sort and never asked whether the
  person has the nature of the predicate (CR3b). It now does; Nestorius's
  "God the Word was born of Mary", with the Word not incarnate, is ill
  typed (CR3), and "Mary is God" is too (CR2d).
- **Bare is monoenergist** (CR6). With no runtime, Christ's humanity has
  no operation and no will: against Constantinople III. The deposit
  defines one created operation, not *agere sequitur esse*; a runtime that
  grants that one alone boots (CR6b).
- **Scripture is read, never raw.** A text enters with readings; no
  branch holds against the reading it takes (CS2c); a reading against a
  definition is excluded (CS2). Of Mk 6:3's three readings, "ever-virgin"
  excludes Helvidius's and leaves Epiphanius's and Jerome's (CS3); without
  the dogma, his reading stands (CS3b). The Fathers' unanimous consent is a
  claim computed both ways, refused on Jn 6:53 once Trent records their
  differing readings (CS4, CS4b).
- **The creeds are earlier than the councils the base cited.** Not
  predicted. The Quicumque and Lateran IV settle the Filioque without
  Lyons II and Florence (CR1, CR1d); typed by its composition, c. 500,
  the Quicumque settles it before Lyons II (CR1b). Its tier-1 standing
  comes from reception, and reception has no date. Paul VI's Credo,
  tier 3, cleaves and does not delete (CR1e).
- **Original sin needs a reading between two definitions.** Not
  predicted. Propagation (DH 1513) reaches Christ unless a reading says
  why it does not (CE1): the virginal conception, or the union. Each
  boots. Mary is preserved by her own definition (DH 2803), "in view of
  the merits of Jesus Christ": a grace before the cross, from it (CE2).
  How causes stand to time is not the deposit's: that rule is a part, and
  the core's order of events need not be a line (CE2c).
  The popular reading, that Christ is free because his mother was, would
  put sin in Christ in the branch Sixtus IV protected: it censures
  Thomas, who held Christ sinless without it (CE1d, CE1e).

### 20. Morality, sin and grace as libraries (MS, GR)

The Prima Secundae closes with two libraries, `moral.lp` and `grace.lp`,
and the canons on Communion call them. Predictions were stamped first
([ttc-moral(7)](research/moral-preregistration.md)).

- **The judgment of persons stays with God.** Mortal sin is composed "on
  the claims": grave matter by teaching about the kind, knowledge and
  consent as claims about the agent (MS1). A law that does not bind makes
  nothing sinful (q. 96 a. 4). That a person is in grace is a claim, never
  derived; read off his reception of the sacraments it is refused, by
  Trent (DH 1534), not only by the checker's rule (GR1).
- **Double effect is legitimate defence's own form** (MS2, MS2b). The
  library and c. 1323 n. 5 read one claim. Proportion is never computed,
  and a good end never makes an act evil by its object licit (MS2c).
- **c. 916 is the internal forum** (MS3): a branch on the claim, in every
  case.
- **c. 915 has two clauses and two natures.** The first reads declared
  penalties only: in the SSPX case it bars the six the 2026 decree names
  once the recourse is decided, and no one while it is pending (MS4,
  MS4b). The second turns on "manifest grave sin". The Pontifical
  Council's objective reading (2000) is not an authentic interpretation,
  so the reading is a doubt of law. Where only the objective reading is
  admissible, outward facts suffice. Where the subjective one is, c. 18
  takes it, the canon restricting the right of c. 912, and the clause
  then reaches only on an interior claim, exactly as c. 916 does (MS5).
- **Bare is incoherent with Trent too.** With no created operation, the
  will moved by grace is "like something inanimate" (GR3). After
  Christ's humanity (§19), the deposit defines a second created
  operation. A runtime that grants either alone boots on its case (CR6b,
  GR3b): the deposit defines instances, not the general thesis.
- **Paul V's protection reaches the library** (GR5). Read in the
  composite sense, Trent's "can dissent" condemns the Thomists; read in
  the divided sense, as the Thomists read it, both schools boot. The
  checker would otherwise have pressed the charge Paul V forbade.
- **Jansen is refused at tier 1** (GR4), on every runtime; the library
  keeps a grace that is resisted.
- **Created and uncreated grace** (GR6). The Palamite runtime fits
  Trent's chapter on the formal cause of justification if it is read
  against Osiander alone, and fails if it is read against every uncreated
  formal cause. The checker computes both readings; the four Latin
  runtimes pass both.

### 21. Christ, presence and the sacraments as libraries (CH, PM, SM)

The *Tertia Pars* gives two libraries, `christ.lp` and `sacrament.lp`; the
canons on absolution, marriage, consecration and the seal now call them.
Predictions and an amendment were stamped first
([ttc-christ(7)](research/christ-preregistration.md)).

- **The person acts, by a nature** (CH1). A human act of the incarnate is
  an *actus humanus* of a divine person; nothing makes him a creature. One
  act typed in both natures is refused (CH1c), and a human act of the
  Father is refused as his birth of Mary is (CH1d).
- **The deposit states two interiors, not one.** Claims composing a sin of
  Christ are refused on DH 301 (CH2). Trent states Mary's freedom from
  venial sin "as the Church holds" (DH 1573), outside any anathema: typed
  tier 3, the same claims are refused in the main branch only (CH2b).
- **Trent warrants typing presence by mode** (PM2). With one untyped
  presence and the rule that a body is in one place, Christ at the right
  hand and in a reserved host turn the deposit red; Trent says there is
  "no repugnance". Whether one body can be circumscribed in two places is
  the schools': red on the Thomist runtime only (PM3).
- **A reformable text read against a definition is outranked, not
  excluded** (PM4). Berengar's profession of 1059, read of the body in its
  own species, stands after Trent as a reading against tier 1. Only
  Scripture's readings are excluded by a definition (inerrancy).
- **Validity, the character and the fruit are three outputs** (SM8). The
  fruit is composed on a claim; for an infant, on the deposit (SM9);
  never as a state of grace. Read off validity it is refused, as
  TTC-MORAL GR1 is.
- **Each condition is typed by its law** (SM2). A dispensation reaches the
  canonical form (c. 1127 §2) and not the water of baptism or the power of
  orders. Consent rests on a tier-3 ground (CCC 1626), so its shadow could
  dispense it. With the substance unclassified, nothing is dispensed and
  nothing refused (SM2b); the Code cited to classify it is law answering
  doctrine (SM2c).
- **Donatism makes every validity a branch on the internal forum** (SM4).
- **"We baptize"** is invalid in the main branch; the valid branch is a
  live shadow of a tier-3 responsum; where the words are in doubt, c. 845
  §2 gives conditional baptism (SM5).
- **Nullity is not dissolution** (SM10). A sentence settles the forum and
  never the fact; read as a divorce, it is refused exactly where the
  marriage was valid and consummated (SM10c).
- **The seal's ground is checked, not cited** (SM7). It rests on tier-3
  teaching: in the shadow where the seal is ecclesiastical law, the
  supreme authority could dispense, and a civil law requiring disclosure
  is not shown void. In the main branch neither. The coerced confessor's
  delict stands, mitigated, without the latae sententiae.

### 22. Verdicts and obligation (VD, OB, DI, SD)

`moral.lp` now says *may*, *must* and *must not*, of acts; the cases the
commentary makes hard, and the Church's social teaching, are its test set.
Predictions were stamped first
([ttc-precept(7)](research/precept-preregistration.md)).

- **Three verdicts and a silence** (VD1). An act of a kind evil by its
  object is "must not" on the kind alone, with nothing claimed; an act that
  passes double effect on the claims is "may"; one that fails only for want
  of a claim gets no verdict. Not shown licit is never reported forbidden.
- **"Must" needs its occasion** (OB1). Affirmative precepts bind always,
  not at every moment: the guard must defend his charges, the bystander
  may. A precept that commands an act evil by its object is refused (OB2).
- **"Direct" is a reading, and the reading meets sources.** By the order
  of causes the push and the loop are direct killings of the innocent; by
  what is chosen they turn on a claim (DI2). The kind is composed from the
  effect, never typed by hand. On the reading by choice, craniotomy and
  salpingostomy still meet the Holy Office's responses (1884, 1889, 1902)
  in every main branch; only their live shadows permit them (DI4, DI5).
  Methotrexate, which no response names, splits by reading. Weight
  prefers the reading by choice, two authors to one, as entered: weight is
  not prediction (§2).
- **Direct abortion is "must not" in every main branch** (DI3). Its contrary
  is a counterfactual shadow on EV 57's claim of the ordinary universal
  magisterium (with tier 2 read as truth), live without it.
- **Circumstance and kind are different grounds** (SD1). As of 2019, on the
  CDF's reading of CCC 2267, an execution is "must not" by circumstance;
  on the intrinsic reading, by kind, with the circumstance main beside it
  (the readings' tension at tier 3, DP3). As of 2017 whether necessity
  existed was open: EV 56's judgment of it is not entered.
- **Usury is two questions** (SD2). Entered as one, the 1917 Code answers
  doctrine; typed as profit from the loan as such and interest by an
  extrinsic title, *Vix pervenit* and c. 1543 both stand.
- **Prudence is input** (SD3). The conditions of a just war are claims of
  those responsible, as proportion is the agent's; no rule derives them.
- **A directive is not a principle** (SD4; CCC 2423).

### 23. Method

- **A fixed relation inventory** makes the grain falsifiable (S8, S9).
- **Donum vitae** rests on A4a. A4a has a warrant from before 1987 (Pius
  XII, 1951, 1956; HV 14).
- **Mutation testing**: every rule, deleted, should turn a test red. This
  caught a vacuous test (T2), four untested canon-law rules, and one bug: an
  ambiguous source with no readings made the whole run unsatisfiable. The
  canon layer had one more, the record of a public claim of necessity,
  found when the library was added (AC12). It now holds for the canon
  layer, the library and the runtimes; 13 statements of the grammar core
  still fail it, down from 46 ([ttc-layers(7)](layers.md)).
- **Non-diagnosis is a test.** No rule derives `interior/3`
  (`test_interior_is_never_derived`).
- **Non-diagnosis is a type rule.** The Dicastery's own return procedure
  (2026) states it.
