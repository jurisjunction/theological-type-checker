# TTC-MENDACIUM(7)

## NAME

**ttc-mendacium**: lying, the first case run alone whose report depends
on the form of the law and on self-preservation; Augustine, Thomas, Kant's
three works, the Catechism, Grotius, Constant and Hobbes, each argument
typed by the causes it rests on, over every runtime and every horizon;
three readings, Augustine's words as given signs, Kant's humanity as an
end, and the end of speech as an end of human nature; the bridge, and the
runtime that can carry Kant's Doctrine of Virtue both ways; with it, every
one of the ten cause parts decides some report of the four cases.
Predictions, committed before encoding.

## DESCRIPTION

Read as the rules stand at `66b90e6` (`main`, TTC-VERBUM encoded and
scored). If `main`'s rules differ from these when encoding begins, the
difference is stated in SCORING.

- **The form of the law has decided nothing.** `form_of_law`, (formal,
  reason), is the one part modernity adds rather than traces
  (TTC-MODERNITY). `kant`, `kant_humanity` and `kant_mm` hold it, and no
  argument of any case rests on it. On the property case alone the two
  forms are idle (PR8), and `kant` falls in Hobbes's group; on `pendet` it
  hangs the law on nothing, as Hobbes does. Over `horizons_all`, three of
  the cases that type arguments by the causes, the first of each record
  that made them (`modernity_property`, `pendet`, `soap`), each run alone,
  have eight order parameters between them, never
  `form_of_law` or `self_preservation`, and put `kant` and `kant_mm` in one
  phase with `hobbes`, `hobbes_wl`, `spinoza` and `spinoza_wl`.
- **PH3 is owed.** TTC-MODERNITY committed every case added to its study,
  lying among them, to a phase diagram predicted in its own preregistration,
  "once its typing is settled and before stamping", and named Kant's
  arguments on lying as the place the form of the law enters.
- **Lying has no case.** CCC 2483's clause on the hearer's right is in
  `linguae_ccc2483` as encodings and a question,
  `q(lie_needs_right_hearer)`, with no argument. The record for lying that
  `linguae-sources.md` cites, `cases-usury-lying.md`, was never committed.
- **`kant_mm`'s end of nature decides nothing.** Its warrant is the
  Doctrine of Virtue's appeal to an end of nature; on the three cases run
  alone it shares Hobbes's phase. The Doctrine of Virtue's article on lying
  appeals to "der natürlichen Zweckmäßigkeit" of the faculty of speech in
  the passage that calls the lie the throwing away of human dignity (TL §9,
  6:429): an end of nature and the humanity in one's person, side by side.

The discussion that found this: what a phase diagram of modernity over
the ten cause parts would need. A case for the form of the law came first.

The proposal:

- **A case, `mendacium`.** One question, whether every lie is wrong;
  nine sources at their tiers; sixteen arguments, each typed by the causes
  it rests on (TTC-MODERNITY, CV).
- **Three readings,** as switches of the case: `ench22_as_given`,
  Augustine's instituted words read by his own account of the given sign;
  `humanity_as_end`, Kant's humanity read as a final end, the reading
  `kant_humanity` makes of the runtime, here made of the arguments;
  `speech_as_human_end`, the end of speech read as an end of human nature
  rather than of a natural thing. The last two are HYPOTHETICAL (rule 6):
  no claimant is entered for them.
- **No rule, slot, runtime or part is added.**

This file is committed and stamped before any of it is encoded. SCORING
will be appended below; nothing above it will be edited.

- **The end of speech, a natural thing's.** TTC-MODERNITY's `nature` is
  "non-rational things, their ends and forms". The record reads the end of
  speech there with its texts: for Thomas voices are "signa naturaliter
  intellectuum", and the lie "innaturale"; Kant's natural purposiveness is
  of a faculty of the physical being, which is not to be used as a mere
  "Sprachmaschine" unbound to "den inneren Zweck (der Gedankenmitteilung)";
  CCC 2485's "purpose of speech" is typed with them, apart from 2486's
  society. The other reading, the end of speech as the inclination of
  rational nature to live in society (`ends_of_human_nature`'s warrant,
  I-II q. 94 a. 2), is `speech_as_human_end`.
- **Augustine's institution, and Grotius's.** *Ench.* 22 says that words
  "sunt instituta", not by whom, and the record types its end with
  Thomas's and the Catechism's end of speech; De doctrina II.2.3's end of
  the sign-giver is the reading `ench22_as_given`. Grotius says that men
  willed the obligation when they "instituerunt" speech, without which the
  invention "inane fuisset": the end willed for it, (final, will), beside
  the pact itself, the will's act, as his property is in
  `modernity_property`.
- **The other typings follow the base.** The act's species from its
  object is (formal, will), as `object_as_form` is defined (VS 78);
  humanity as an end in itself is the form of the law, as `kant` reads
  Kant, against the reading `kant_humanity` makes; a law and its absence
  by the sovereign's will are (efficient, will), as `hobbes_l26` is in
  `pendet`.
- **The works are the sources.** Kant's three works are three sources, as
  Thomas's articles are in the property case. `th110_ad4` rests on what
  the corpus rests on: its reply grounds the sin in the lie's own disorder
  "ut dictum est", and the theft illustrates that ground. Its two
  arguments share a typing, so `th110` is never reported.

- **`ench22_as_given`.** *De doctrina christiana* II.2.3: there is no cause
  of giving a sign but to bring what is in the giver's mind into another's.
  So read, the end for which words were instituted is the end of the
  sign-giver's will: `reading_of(ench22_given, ench22_words)`,
  (final, will).
- **`humanity_as_end`** (HYPOTHETICAL). G 4:429 and TL 6:429 read with
  humanity a final end of human nature, against their author:
  `reading_of(g429_end, g429)`, `reading_of(tl_dignity_end, tl_dignity)`,
  (final, human).
- **`speech_as_human_end`** (HYPOTHETICAL). The end of speech read as an
  end of human nature: further readings of `ench22_words`, `tl_purpose`
  and `ccc2485_speech` on (final, human), and of `th110_genus` and
  `th110_ad4` on (formal, will) and (final, human).

A further reading only adds availability (TTC-MODERNITY, CV4).

  runtime but `bare`; `grotius_iii1_11` is so with or without it.

## CAVEATS

- **The typings are the interpretation.** The end of speech is typed by
  nature, as a natural thing's, with the texts above; by the inclination of
  rational nature it would be `speech_as_human_end`, under which the
  Thomists' phase takes in the middle and `kant_humanity` bridges as
  `kant_mm` does (MW3, MV3). Thomas's "naturaliter" stands beside his own
  "ex institutione humana" (*In Peri herm.* I lect. 2 n. 4): in lect. 4
  the voice is "quaedam res naturalis" and the name "ab hominibus
  institutum" (n. 4, in a difficulty), signifying "secundum institutionem
  humanam" (n. 6). Constant's correlation of duty and right is not entered
  as a premise: his argument rests on the society it would make
  impossible.
- **The unit of a source decides `sole_reason`.** Were Kant's three works
  one source, `kant` would report nothing; the Thomists would still hear
  `tl_purpose` alone. The record's sources are works.
- **Nothing reported is not everything followed.** The middle follows
  neither Thomas nor Kant and reports nothing (MN5). Where the will's ends
  are held, no keeps Grotius's reason, and Constant's and Hobbes's
  arguments change no report; where both values have a reason, the
  checker says nothing.
- **A horizon is not a conclusion.** Under `ench22_as_given` Hobbes's
  horizon follows Augustine's argument; Hobbes counts the lie an abuse of
  speech (*Leviathan* 4) and fraud a virtue in war (*Leviathan* 13). What
  a horizon can follow is computed; what a figure concludes is not.
- **Kant's scope is disputed.** Whether Kant forbids every false
  statement (Akimoto: the lie against duty, the untruth not), whether the
  essay of 1797 is a doctrine of right (Varden), and how §9 is ordered
  (Bacin and Schönecker) are not computed. The case types his arguments,
  not the reach of his conclusion. His argument in the essay that whoever
  lies "muß die Folgen davon … verantworten" is about imputation and is not
  entered (rule 3). "Menschheit überhaupt" in the essay is mankind, not
  the formula of humanity, so `humanity_as_end` does not read `vrml`.
- **Lying, not concealment.** Thomas allows the truth to be hidden
  prudently (ad 4); mental reservation, wide and strict, is not typed.
- **Grotius's "forte".** He allows the falsehood to save an innocent life
  only perhaps (XVI); infants, madmen, the one not deceived, the one
  content to be, and a superior right (XII–XV) are no lie for him without
  it. He supports no on those.
- **Scripture is not entered.** The midwives, Abraham, Jacob and Judith
  (II-II q. 110 a. 3 obj. 2–3, ad 2–3) are read both ways, and the case
  enters no tier 0. Jn 8:44 enters through CCC 2482.
- **The hearer's right is not joined.** `grotius_iii1_11` and
  `constant_viii` rest on the right of the one addressed; the 1992 text of
  CCC 2483 gave that right a place in the definition, and the Latin of
  1997 leaves it to the norms on communicating the truth, as TTC-LINGUAE
  reads the change ("Ius ad communicationem veritatis absolutum non est",
  2488). Joining `q(lie_needs_right_hearer)` to this question is a later
  step.
- **Tiers and dates.** Augustine and Thomas enter at 4 by promotion, as in
  `pendet`; the Catechism at 3, dated 1992, as `ccc2403` is, and not
  withdrawn in 1997 as `ccc_ed1992` is in `linguae_ccc2483`: its 2482 and
  2485 are the same in both editions, and 2486 as far as the second is
  reached. No prediction reads the date under the default clock.
- **Expected and risky.** MN1 is expected to hold. MN2, MH, MW, MV and MJ
  rest on the two plain models; a difference between them and
  `causae.lp` would show there first. MC1 rests on the authority rules'
  reading of two cases at once. SU1's restated counts are the likeliest
  to be wrong.

## BUGS

- The report vocabulary is blind to a value reasoned on both sides and to
  a source with one argument: where the will's ends are held, Constant's
  and Hobbes's arguments change no report, and the middle, which follows
  neither Thomas nor Kant, reports nothing. A report that names the
  arguments a horizon can follow on each side is not proposed here.
