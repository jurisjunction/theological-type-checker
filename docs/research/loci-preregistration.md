# TTC-LOCI(7)

## NAME

**ttc-loci**: the schools' readings as alleles at loci, carried and
expressed; a substitution for God, entered as a claim, faulting on the
deposit; the icon against the idol. Predictions, committed before encoding.

## DESCRIPTION

Read as the rules stand at `c2cd56a` (`main`, TTC-MODERNITY merged):

- **A runtime inherits everything.** L unions its parents' parts and
  refuses a child only where one parent has a part the other excludes.
  The schools do not exclude each other's readings; each adds its own.
  So `suarez_dm23`, a child of `suarezian` and of `scotist` through
  `scotus_dpp`, holds the Thomist simplicity and the Scotist formal
  distinction of the attributes at once. It refuses `composition_in_god`
  on its own, and so do the twenty runtimes below it (TTC-MODERNITY,
  SCORING, Not predicted). Hobbes, on this inheritance, holds a view on
  Scotus's formal distinction.
- **The pairs of TTC-SCHOOLS** show where the schools' readings clash:
  every clash falls on the simplicity whitelist, the principle of
  distinction, or Th. 5 (P9). Two of these are readings of one question
  each, simplicity and esse. The third lies on `one_power`, which
  `palamite` excludes, and L already refuses a child that inherits both.
- **The deposit has no rule for the last end or for latria.** It
  defines that God alone gives being (Lateran IV, DH 800) and that
  creatures are contingent (DH 3002, 3025), and the kernel refuses a
  contingent thing taken as necessary. Nothing refuses a creature taken
  as the last end, or given the worship due to God.
- **A trace and a substitution cannot be told apart.** TTC-MODERNITY's
  trace loses reasons. A horizon that puts a creature in God's place is
  not a trace. It is "a partial trace taken as the whole" (the roadmap,
  on heresy). The base has no slot for it.

The discussion that found this: the review of TTC-MODERNITY's finding,
which read intellectual history as one whole, each horizon a kind of
trace of it, below which the deposit stands as the layer every runtime
runs on.

The proposal:

- A school's reading of one question is an allele at a locus. An
  allele's parts travel together.
- A child whose parents express different alleles at a locus carries
  them all and expresses none, unless it says which. Silent, it runs on
  the deposit's definition alone. A carried allele can be expressed by a
  descendant.
- A substitution is entered as a claim, with its claimant, of a text or an
  order, never of a person. Read so, it is lowered onto the deposit's own
  relations, and faults there. A pure trace faults nowhere.
- The deposit gains two rules, each what a tier-1 text defines: the last
  end of the rational creature is God (*Benedictus Deus*), and latria is
  due to God alone (Nicaea II).
- Honour is typed by where it terminates. An icon's honour passes to its
  prototype, and never faults when that is God or a saint honoured as a
  saint. An idol's stops at the sign.

This file is committed and stamped before any of it is encoded. SCORING
will be appended below; nothing above it will be edited.

## THE DESIGN

### Loci (`l.md`, `schools.l`)

- **A locus** is declared with its alleles. An allele is a set of parts
  that travel together:

  ```
  locus simplicity
    allele by_reason  = simplicity_thomist, attributes_by_reason
    allele formal     = simplicity_scotist, attributes_formal
    allele energetic  = simplicity_palamite
    allele synonymous = simplicity_synonymous
  locus esse
    allele real       = th03, th05
    allele by_reason  = suarez_esse
    allele scotus     = scotus_esse
  ```

- **Expressed.** A runtime expresses an allele when it holds all its
  parts. A part declared at two loci, an allele with a part that is not a
  part, and a runtime holding some of an allele's parts and not the rest
  do not compile.
- **Inheritance at a locus**, computed by L at build:
  - If the parents express one allele, the child expresses it.
  - If they express two or more, the child carries them all and
    expresses none: their parts are not loaded. It runs on the deposit's
    simplicity (`allowed(defined, …)`) and the deposit's silence on
    esse. Nothing is refused.
  - `express A` expresses an allele the child inherits, expressed or
    carried, and carries the rest. An `express` of an allele it does not
    inherit does not compile. Expressing an allele that every parent only
    carries is reported (`re_expressed`).
  - `carry A` silences an allele the child would express: its parts are
    not loaded, and it passes on as carried. A `carry` of an allele the
    child would not express does not compile.
  - Carried alleles are inherited as carried. `runtimes.json` records,
    for each runtime and locus, the allele expressed and the alleles
    carried.
- **A pair is not a child.** TTC-SCHOOLS's pairs load two runtimes whole,
  and their clashes stand.
- **The resolutions**, each with its text. No other runtime says
  anything; the rest follows.
  - `suarez_dm23` and `suarez_wl`: `express by_reason` at both loci (DM
    7.1.4–5, 30.3.4–5, 31.1.12–13), as the base's `suarezian` runtime
    reads Suárez.
  - `ockham`: `carry formal` at simplicity. He denies the formal
    distinction of the attributes "universally" (*Ord.* I d. 2 q. 1).
    His own reading, the attributes as terms, is left.
  - `hobbes`, and its twin `hobbes_wl`: `carry by_reason` at simplicity.
    The attributes signify "Pious Intention", not "Philosophicall Truth"
    (*Leviathan* 31). His own reading, the attributes as honours, is
    left.

### Substitution (`substitutio.l`)

- **The slot.** `substitutes(C, T, X, Y, R)`: the claimant C reads the
  text or order T as putting X in the place of Y, in the role R. Sort
  `role` {giver_of_being, last_end}.
- **Necessity, beside it.** `necessitates(C, T, X)`: C reads T as holding
  the creature X necessary. It is no substitution: a text can deny free
  creation without putting anything in God's place.
- **Lowered onto the deposit's relations.** `giver_of_being` gives
  `being(X, Z)` for every creature Z other than X; `last_end` gives
  `last_end(X)`; `necessitates` gives `necessary(X)`. The deposit's rules
  then decide: `being_from_non_god` (DH 800), the new `last_end_not_god`
  (DH 1000), and the kernel's `modal_collapse` on the deposit's
  `contingent/1` (DH 3002, 3025).
- **The report.** `info(substitution_faults(C, T, X, V, L))` and
  `info(necessity_faults(C, T, X, V, L))`: read by C, T faults on the
  rule V, of the layer L (deposit or kernel, as `docs/layers.csv` labels
  it).
- **A claim, computed both ways.** A substitution entered as disputed
  runs in a branch with it and a branch without.
- **Never of a person** (rule 3). A substitution or an honour entered of
  a person is refused (`substitution_of_a_person(T)`), as a hearing of a
  person is (TTC-LINGUAE). The checker types a text or an order, never a
  soul.

### Honour (`substitutio.l`, and the deposit)

- **The slot.** `honour(S, P, K, C)`: by C's reading, honour of the kind
  K given to the sign S terminates in P. Sort `honour_kind` {latria,
  veneration, hyperdulia, dulia}. An icon's P is its prototype; an
  idol's P is the sign itself.
- **The deposit's rule** (Nicaea II, DH 601):
  `latria_to_creature(P)`, where latria terminates in what is not God.
  Honour that terminates in God faults nothing, through an image or not:
  "the honour paid to the image passes to the prototype".
- **Two readings of one honour.** Nicaea II calls the honour of an image
  of Christ veneration, not "true latria". Thomas calls it latria, since
  "the motion to the image as image is one and the same with the motion
  to the thing", and gives the image as a thing "nulla reverentia" (III
  q. 25 a. 3). "Relative latria" is the Thomists' term, entered with them
  as claimant. Both readings enter, and both terminate in Christ.

### The deposit (`kernel_core.l`)

Two rules, labelled `deposit` in `docs/layers.csv`, each with its text:

- `last_end_not_god(X)`: X taken as the last end of the rational creature,
  and X is not God (*Benedictus Deus*, DH 1000–1001; *Dei Filius*, DH
  3025).
- `latria_to_creature(P)`: latria terminating in what is not God (Nicaea
  II, DH 601).

### The cases (`icon_history.l`)

Real texts; every substitution and honour is a reading with its claimant.

- **`loci_spinoza`.** *Ethics* I p14, p29, p33; IV Praef., "God or
  Nature". Two readings, disputed, so both run. On A, the pantheist (Jacobi,
  1785; Borge 2025), nature is put in God's place as the giver of being,
  and held necessary. On B (Nadler 2002), God is *natura naturans*, and
  the things that follow from him, *natura naturata*, are held necessary
  (I p29, p33). On either, what is not God is a creature, as *Dei Filius*
  has it (DH 3001).
- **`loci_mammon`.** McCarraher's reading of the capitalist order:
  money "occupies the ontological throne from which God has been
  evicted". One substitution, money for God as the last end. Thomas makes
  avarice like idolatry by a likeness, not by worship, so it enters as the
  last end and not as latria.
- **`loci_icon`.** An image of Christ, under Nicaea II's reading
  (veneration) and Thomas's (latria, terminating in Christ); an image of a saint
  (dulia, terminating in the saint); and an idol, latria terminating in
  the sign.
- **`loci_adams`.** *Education* XXV, two readings. Adams's own is
  TTC-MODERNITY's measure (`modernity_measure`), unchanged. McCarraher's
  enters as honour: the dynamo as "a graven image", latria terminating in
  the dynamo.
- **`cand_substitution_person`** (HYPOTHETICAL): a substitution entered
  of a person.

## PREDICTIONS

### LO: the loci

- **LO1.** L compiles every runtime of the base. It refuses, in tests on
  toy sources: a part at two loci; an allele with a part that is not a
  part; a runtime holding part of an allele; an `express` of an allele not
  inherited. It reports `re_expressed` where a child expresses an allele
  every parent only carries.
- **LO2.** As built, at **simplicity**:
  - `by_reason`: `thomist`, `suarezian`, `hooker`, `suarez_dm23` and
    `suarez_wl` (by `express`), and every runtime below them but
    `hobbes` and `hobbes_wl`;
  - `formal`: `scotist`, `scotus_dpp`, `scotus_will`;
  - `energetic`: `palamite`;
  - none, carrying `formal`: `ockham`, `ockham_will`, `ockham_nominal`;
  - none, carrying `by_reason` and `formal`: `hobbes`, `hobbes_wl`;
  - none, carrying nothing: `bare`, `default`, `bacon`, `saussure`,
    `vocal`, `lingual`, `marx`, `marx_social`, `lassalle`.
- **LO3.** At **esse**:
  - `real`: `thomist`, `hooker`, `episcopate_1891`, `social_catholic`,
    `angers`;
  - `scotus`: `scotist`, `scotus_dpp`, `ockham` and its readings,
    `scotus_will`;
  - `by_reason`: `suarezian`, `suarez_dm23`, `suarez_wl`, and every other
    runtime below them, save `angers` (above) and those that follow;
  - none, carrying `real`, `by_reason` and `scotus`: `locke`, `locke_wl`,
    `smith`, `george`, `george_hn`;
  - none, carrying `by_reason` and `scotus`: `hobbes`, `hobbes_wl`;
  - none, carrying nothing: as at simplicity, with `palamite`.
- **LO4.** No runtime refuses `composition_in_god` or
  `received_esse_without_real_distinction`, on its own or on the property
  case. The twenty-one runtimes of TTC-MODERNITY's finding lose exactly
  `composition_in_god`, and nothing else changes in any scenario (every
  scenario's atoms compared, before and after, in every model).
- **LO5.** The four schools express their own alleles and are unchanged,
  so TTC-SCHOOLS's boot and pair tables are unchanged (P1–P6 stand).
- **LO6.** No runtime of the base re-expresses a carried allele.

### SB: substitution

- **SB1.** `loci_spinoza`, on every runtime, `spinoza` and `bare`
  included, since no runtime excludes the deposit:
  - on A, `being_from_non_god(natura, …)` (deposit) and
    `modal_collapse(natura)` (kernel, on the deposit's contingency);
  - on B, `modal_collapse(natura_naturata)` only. No substitution
    faults: B puts nothing in God's place, and still denies free
    creation.
- **SB2.** `loci_mammon` faults on `last_end_not_god(money)` (deposit), on
  every runtime. Without the new rule it faults nowhere: the deposit had
  no rule for it.
- **SB3.** A pure trace faults nowhere. On every runtime, with the
  property case, under every switch and every hearing, no violation fires
  (with LO4).
- **SB4.** The two readings of *Education* XXV fall on two layers.
  Adams's measure is refused at TTC-MODERNITY's library, save where
  native (CV5, unchanged). McCarraher's idol faults on the deposit,
  `latria_to_creature(dynamo)`, on every runtime, `hobbes` included.
- **SB5.** A substitution or an honour entered of a person is refused
  (`substitution_of_a_person`).

### IC: the icon and the idol

- **IC1.** The image of Christ faults nothing, on Nicaea II's reading and
  on Thomas's: the honour terminates in Christ. The image of a saint,
  dulia terminating in the saint, faults nothing.
- **IC2.** The idol faults: latria terminating in the sign
  (`latria_to_creature`).
- **IC3.** The terminus decides, not the sign: the same image given
  latria that terminates in itself faults, and given latria that passes to
  Christ does not.

### SU: the suite

- **SU1.** No expectation changes but LO4's. CL1 and PR4 stay clean.
- **SU2.** Every new rule and slot has a test that fails without it. The
  two deposit rules are labelled `deposit` in `docs/layers.csv`, and
  `docs/layers.md`'s summary is updated.
- **SU3.** `docs/encoding-guide.md` and `guide.md` gain the vocabulary
  identically.

### EX: reported, not predicted

- **EX1.** The thirty-three runtimes of TTC-MODERNITY, now free of the

## CAVEATS

- **Silence is not a view.** A figure who carries two alleles is not
  said to hold the deposit's simplicity against the schools. He holds
  none of their readings, which is what the record documents of most
  modern figures.
- **Canon 1.3 is left.** *Dei Filius* condemns one substance of God and
  of all things (DH 3023), and Spinoza's I p14 falls under it on both
  readings. The deposit has no rule for it yet; this step enters only
  what the deposit already decides, and the two rules above.
- **Ockham's and Hobbes's own readings are left.** Each now carries the
  allele he would have inherited, and expresses none. Neither the
  attributes as terms nor the attributes as honours is yet an allele.
- **Inherited expression is unexamined.** Spinoza expresses Suárez's
  distinction of reason by inheritance through Descartes, and Locke
  Thomas's simplicity through Hooker, Descartes and Grotius. The records
  have no text on either. They are marked as TTC-MODERNITY marks its
  inherited values.
- **A substitution is a reading of a text or an order,** never of a
  person (rule 3). The checker shows that, read so, the text faults on
  the deposit. It does not show that the reading is right: Spinoza's
  defenders deny it, and both readings run.
- **The deposit grows by two definitions,** and only by what each defines.
  The last end of the rational creature is God, seen; latria is due to
  God alone. Neither decides a school question.
- **Mammon by likeness.** Thomas compares avarice to idolatry by a
  likeness, "for use, not worship". So money enters as the last end, not
  as latria, and the idol of the step is McCarraher's dynamo.
- **Genetics is an analogy, and only that.** Loci, alleles, linkage and
  carrying name a bookkeeping of inheritance. There is no fitness and no
  selection here: the DAG is the record's, and a reading survives by being
  documented, not by winning.
