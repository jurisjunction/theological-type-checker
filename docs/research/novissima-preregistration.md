# TTC-NOVISSIMA(7)

## NAME

**ttc-novissima** — the last things: the order after death given by
definitions and never measured by the calendar; the vision deferred and
defined; the end arriving early; no one's state computed; what lasts; one
sacrifice in many times; predictions, committed before encoding

## DESCRIPTION

Read as the rules stand (the base run as committed, 66d859f, only for the
outputs quoted here):

- **The economy ends, and is Christ's.** The kernel's order runs from
  creation to the return and the judgment (`creed`); `death`,
  `resurrection` and the rest are his. The end is never dated (OR2) and
  never past (OR2b). No person's death, judgment, purification, vision or
  rising is in the base, and no order of them.
- **Measures for kinds, not states.** Eternity for God, the aevum for
  angels (Thomas's), time for every other creature (MD1). A separated soul
  is no sort; "eternal life" is not typed. Nothing refuses a calendar laid
  on what follows death.
- **The Assumption settled, and nothing more.** As of today the Assumption
  is settled (DH 3903) and Mary's death before it open, "yes" preferred
  (CM3). Nothing marks it as the end arriving early, and nothing compares
  it with anyone else's rising.
- **Hell, purgatory, the vision, the rising of the body: no question, no
  source.** Lateran IV (1215) is in `creeds` with its Trinitarian supports
  only. Florence's decree of union (DH 1306) enters only as a constraint on
  infants (`limbo`: three ways open, "beatific" preferred). Benedict XII,
  John XXII and Lateran V are not in the record.
- **Interior states are claims** (rule 3): grace is a claim, never derived
  from outward signs (`grace.lp`). Nothing speaks of a state after death,
  and no rule would stop a case from placing a named person in hell. No
  canonization or beatification is in the record.
- **No sacrifice.** The Eucharist is typed by presence (TTC-CHRIST), not as
  the one sacrifice of the cross. No Mass is an event of the order.

This file is committed and stamped before any of it is encoded. SCORING
will be appended below; nothing above it will be edited.

## THE LAYER

Fixed here.

- **An order of stages, given.** The last things of each person are an
  order of stage kinds, not of persons: dying; purification; the vision;
  hell; rising; the general judgment; and the kernel's return. An order
  between two stages is given by a source (`gives_order/3`) and holds while
  the source is in force at tier 1. Benedict XII gives dying before the
  vision ("mox", or after purification), purification before the vision,
  dying before hell ("mox"), the vision before rising ("ante resumptionem
  suorum corporum"), rising before the general judgment; Lateran IV the
  return before rising and the general judgment after; Florence the same
  three ways at death. Lateran IV gives the vision and hell without end
  (`without_end/2`). Two judgments: each soul's destiny at death, all at
  the end. The particular judgment named so is the Catechism's (CCC 1022,
  tier 3) and gives no order of its own.
- **Theories placed on the order.** A theory of what follows death is
  entered as the order it claims (`places/3`) and the stages it denies.
  Placing one stage before another that a definition in force places
  after it is refused (`order_against_definition`), as is ending a stage
  given without end (`end_of_the_endless`). A theory is refused only by
  what is in force at the evaluation date: the order is dated.
- **On the authority graph, the record as it stands.** Where a position
  has a history, it is a question: the vision before the rising
  (`q(vision_before_rising)`) and the end of punishment
  (`q(punishment_ends)`). Sources enter at the tier of their form (rule
  14): John XXII's sermons a pope's ordinary teaching in homilies (tier 3,
  one point), withdrawn by his own act of 1334; *Ne super his* at tier 3;
  *Benedictus Deus* at tier 1, its conditions shown from its words; the
  synod of 543 a particular council (weighs, cleaves nothing).
- **One soul, one body.** All rise "with their own bodies, which they now
  bear" (DH 801); the soul is multiplied according to the bodies it
  informs (DH 1440). A soul given two bodies is refused
  (`soul_in_two_bodies`): reincarnation (CCC 1013). A raising returns a soul
  to its own body (Jn 11); no rule counts deaths.
- **The end arriving early.** Bodily glorification belongs to the rising at
  the return, by the general rule (DH 801; *Munificentissimus Deus* 4).
  Christ's resurrection is its firstfruits (1 Cor 15:20); Mary was
  "exempted from this general rule" (n. 5), and her Assumption anticipates
  the others' (CDF 1979 n. 6; CCC 966). Both are reported
  (`end_early/1`); a rising before the return claimed of anyone else is
  refused (`rising_before_the_return`), as is every rising placed at death.
- **Never measured by the calendar.** What follows death is ordered and
  never measured by the calendar: a duration in days or years laid on
  purification, the vision or hell is refused (Spe salvi 47; *Indulgentiarum
  doctrina* n. 12). The former days of indulgences measured the canonical
  penance of the earth, which they equalled; read as days in purgatory,
  they are refused. The constitution's norms are law: in the canon layer,
  under their `@norm`, in force three months from their issue of the
  *Acta* (computed by c. 8 §1's rule from that date, read at encoding). A
  soul after death, a spiritual creature, carries Thomas's measures (I
  q. 10 a. 5 ad 1), as the angels do: the aevum for its being, a share in
  eternity for the vision of glory. Eternity itself is God's alone.
- **No one's state computed** (rule 3). No rule places a person in hell,
  purification or glory. A case that does is refused
  (`state_after_death_placed`), save glory where the Church's act of
  canonization in force says so, which is reported as the act's. The
  canonization's tier is computed from its formula (rule 14): a papal
  definition, "decernimus et definimus", of a fact connected with
  revelation, tier 2; the 1998 commentary's word on it is a claim of a
  lower tier about a higher, recorded and fixing nothing. A beatification
  permits a cult: governance, never read as teaching.
- **What lasts.** Law and teaching say what binds (TTC-LITURGY). The world
  at the end is a question: transformed, annihilated, or kept unchanged.
  "Nothing of this world carried over" is refused on every branch: Christ's
  flesh, taken from Mary, is seated at the right hand (DH 150, 1636), and
  Mary is in glory body and soul (DH 3903). Whether plants and animals
  remain is a question between Thomas (no) and *Laudato si'* (yes). That
  charity and its fruits endure is GS 39's teaching; which things charity
  carries is interior: a claim, computed both ways, never derived, and
  binding nothing now (LO10).
- **One sacrifice in many times.** A Mass is an event of the order with its
  own date, joined to the one passion (`same_sacrifice/2`), as the Last
  Supper is before it: a join, not an order edge, and no second passion. A
  Mass typed as a second immolation, or as a bare commemoration, is
  refused (Trent, sess. XXII).
- **A new library**, `novissima.lp`: the stages, the order given, the
  theories placed on it, the measures after death, one soul one body, the
  end early, no one's state, what lasts. The sacrifice in `sacrament.lp`;
  the indulgences' norms in `canon_law.lp`.

## METHOD

Write `novissima.lp`. In `sacrament.lp`: the one sacrifice. In
`canon_law.lp`: *Indulgentiarum doctrina*, norm 4 and the transitional
norms, under `@norm`. In `saeculum.lp`, if needed: a measure for what
follows death. Data in `icon_history.lp`, as parts loaded beside the
records they speak to, so that no earlier scenario changes: the order
after death (with `creeds`); the vision before the rising, John XXII to
Florence; the end of punishment, 543 to 1215; the world at the end; plants
and animals. Cases, all HYPOTHETICAL: the theories placed on the order
(the vision deferred, sleep, total death, rising at death, restoration of
all); a soul in two bodies and a raising; another's rising before the
return; a duration in days laid on purification, and a grant of days read
both ways; a named person placed in hell, in purification and in glory; a
canonization and a beatification; nothing of this world carried over; an
element claimed to last because it is loved; a Mass, the Last Supper, a
second immolation and a bare commemoration. Run the whole suite, the
runtimes' boots and the coverage index.

## PREDICTIONS

### NV: the order after death

- **NV1.** The order given, as of today: dying before the vision, before
  purification and before hell; purification before the vision; the vision
  before rising; the return before rising; rising before the general
  judgment; the vision and hell without end. Each reported with its source,
  none below tier 1.
- **NV2.** Theories placed on the order. Sleep until the rising (no vision
  before it): refused from 1336, not as of 1300. Rising at death, for all:
  refused from 1215 (the return before rising, DH 801), not as of 1200.
  Total death (no soul subsists; all re-created at the
  end): refused from 1336 on the order and from 1513 by Lateran V's
  immortality; as of 1300, not refused. The vision before purification, for
  one who needs it: refused from 1336.
- **NV3.** One soul, one body. A soul given a second body after death
  (reincarnation): refused (`soul_in_two_bodies`). A raising to the same
  body, followed by a second death (Jn 11): not refused, and nothing is
  computed of the person.
- **NV4.** The end early. Mary's Assumption reported as the end arriving
  early (`end_early(mary)`), on every branch from 1950, with no refusal of
  the end as past (OR2b keeps its output); Christ's resurrection reported
  as the firstfruits (`end_early(son)`). A bodily rising before the return
  claimed of another: refused from 1215 (`rising_before_the_return`), not
  as of 1200.
- **NV5.** The end of punishment. As of 1000: "punishment ends" open, never
  on a branch where the *Quicumque* is received (a claim, TTC-SAECULUM),
  and "no" preferred by the synod of 543's weight; the synod cleaves
  nothing. From 1215: "yes" on no branch (Lateran IV). The restoration of
  all placed on the order (hell ending in the vision): refused from 1215
  (`end_of_the_endless`), not as of 1000.

### JX: the vision deferred

- **JX1.** As of 1332: the vision deferred is main; "the vision before the
  rising" a live shadow of John XXII's sermons, reintegrating if they are
  relaxed. Thomas weighs for it at tier 5.
- **JX2.** As of 1335: the sermons withdrawn by his own act; *Ne super
  his* holds the vision before the rising at tier 3; the deferral a live
  shadow of it.
- **JX3.** From 1336: the deferral on no branch (*Benedictus Deus*,
  warranted at tier 1 by its own words, no condition unshown). As of 1440
  Florence holds the same at tier 1, and the question's classification
  does not change. The deferral placed on the order as a theory is refused
  from 1336, not as of 1335 (NV2's rule).

### DU: never measured by the calendar

- **DU1.** A duration in days laid on purification, on the vision or on
  hell: refused (`after_death_measured`). A year laid on the rising is
  refused as the return's is (`end_dated`, OR2's rule).
- **DU2.** A grant of 300 days (1950): its days reported as a measure of
  canonical penance on earth; the same grant read as 300 days in
  purgatory, refused (DU1's rule).
- **DU3.** *Indulgentiarum doctrina* in the canon layer, its norms in force
  three months from its issue of the *Acta*. A partial indulgence granted
  with days in 1970: contrary to norm 4; the grant of 1950: not. The grant
  of 1950, unconfirmed: in force as of 1968, null as of 1970 (two years
  from the constitution's date). Neither law answers how long purification
  lasts.
- **DU4.** Thomas's measures for a spiritual creature (I q. 10 a. 5 ad 1),
  carried as his, as the angels' aevum is: a soul after death
  (`separated_soul/1`) measured by the aevum in its natural being, nothing
  refused on it; MD1 keeps its output. The blessed said measured by
  eternity itself: refused (`eternal_with_beginning`), as light's was
  (MD3). The vision typed as a share in eternity (I q. 10 a. 3, "vitam
  aeternam"): not refused.

## CAVEATS

The order is of stage kinds: no person is placed in it. The particular
judgment as a stage, the "immediately" of DH 1000 as excluding any stage
between death and the soul's destiny, and the duration of hell's
punishment of the senses are not encoded. Lyons II's profession (DH
856–859) was read only through CCC 1059: it waits. So does the
glorified body's mode ("not limited by space and time", CCC 645, or in a
place, Thomas): presence stays the christ library's.

John XXII's sermons enter at the tier of their form; that he did not mean
to decide is his own later word, a claim about his own act (rule 14), and
changes no tier. Their withdrawal is entered at 1334, by the consistory and
*Ne super his* together. *Benedictus Deus*'s fifth condition (the whole
Church) is read from its words at encoding; if it is not shown, JX3 is
false.

The canonization is HYPOTHETICAL, in the words of the formula as
pronounced today; the formula's history, and canonizations before it took
this form, wait. Its conditions are shown by its own words, apart from the
1998 commentary; that the canonization's object is a fact connected with

reported; the fifteen anathemas of 553 and their ascription to the fifth

here: "Damnation remains a real possibility, but it is not granted to us
