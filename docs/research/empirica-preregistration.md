# TTC-EMPIRICA(7)

## NAME

**ttc-empirica** — the empirical kind of question: no tier of teaching
settles one, a finding does, and a finding settles no doctrine; Galileo
(1616, 1633) and the Big Bang (1951) as the two directions of the same
error; method never answers being; how many human beings, a fact about
souls computed both ways; predictions, committed before encoding

## DESCRIPTION

Read as the rules stand (the base run as committed, 4da10a2, only for the
outputs quoted here):

- **Two kinds of question.** A question is doctrinal or governance
  (`question_kind/2`). A source at tier 1 deletes the contrary branches
  and one at tier 2 or 3 cleaves them, whatever the question: a question
  marked `empirical` today is settled by a hypothetical act at tier 1 as a
  doctrine would be.
- **No finding.** Nothing in the base records what the sciences find. A
  reading of Scripture is excluded only by a tier-1 source or a defined
  sense.
- **The Fathers' consent** (`consent_claim/2`) is computed both ways and
  refused where an act in force records diverse readings (Jn 6:53). It
  binds only where the text is marked as of faith or morals
  (`faith_morals/1`); outside them it fixes nothing, and nothing says so.
  It has no claimant.
- **Not in the record:** Galileo's case (1616, 1633, 1820), Pius XII's
  address of 1951, the Council of Vienne (DH 902), *Humani generis* 36,
  *Donum vitae*, the Code of 1917's c. 748.
- **One human being.** The kernel world has twinning shapes for organisms
  (`gen(budding, …)`, `gen(fission, …)`); a twinning of persons entered so
  passes silently. A soul given by a creature is refused
  (`being_from_non_god`, A1; `cand_traducianism`). No rule speaks of a
  soul divided, of one soul in two organisms, or of how many human beings
  there are.

This file is committed and stamped before any of it is encoded. SCORING
will be appended below; nothing above it will be edited.

## THE TEXTS

Fixed here. Read on vatican.va where it holds them; Denzinger's numbering
otherwise. The fetch returned some long passages in pieces; each is to be
read whole at encoding.

- **Trent**, sess. IV (1546, DH 1507): no one may interpret Scripture "in
  rebus fidei, et morum ad aedificationem doctrinae christianae
  pertinentium ... contra unanimem consensum patrum"; renewed by Vatican I
  (*Dei Filius* 2). *Dei Filius* 4 (DH 3017): "nulla tamen umquam inter
  fidem et rationem vera dissensio esse potest"; "nec verum vero umquam
  contradicere" (CCC 159).
- **Leo XIII, *Providentissimus Deus*** (1893) 18: "There can never, indeed,
  be any real discrepancy between the theologian and the physicist, as long
  as each confines himself within his own lines"; the sacred writers "did
  not seek to penetrate the secrets of nature, but rather described and
  dealt with things in more or less figurative language, or in terms which
  were commonly used at the time". **DV 11**: the books teach "that truth
  which God wanted put into sacred writings for the sake of salvation".
- **Josh 10:12–13** (NAB): "And the sun stood still, and the moon
  stayed"; its note: "described in popular language and according to
  external appearances".
- **The censure of 24 February 1616** (the Holy Office's theologians;
  unpublished; Pagano 2009, after ASV Misc. Arm. X 204 f. 42r): the sun
  immobile at the centre "stultam et absurdam in Philosophia; et
  formaliter haereticam, quatenus contradicit expresse sententiis sacrae
  scripturae in multis locis, secundum proprietatem verborum, et secundum
  communem expositionem, et sensum, Sanctorum Patrum et Theologorum
  doctorum"; the earth's motion "ad minus esse in fide erroneam". Paul V
  then had Bellarmine warn Galileo (minutes of 25 February).
- **The decree of the Congregation of the Index** (5 March 1616), published
  by the pope's order: "falsam illam doctrinam Pithagoricam, divinaeque
  scripturae omnino adversantem"; Copernicus "suspendendos esse donec
  corrigantur"; Foscarini "omnino prohibendum". Its form is disputed: "this
  tribunal being absolutely incompetent to make a dogmatic decree", the
  pope's approval "in forma communi" (*Catholic Encyclopedia*, "Galileo");
  "non fu certo una 'misura disciplinare', ma un decreto dottrinale"
  (Fantoli).
- **The sentence of 22 June 1633** (seven cardinals, Inquisitors General;
  not signed by the pope): Galileo "vehementemente sospetto d'heresia",
  for holding a doctrine "falsa e contraria alle Sacre e divine Scritture"
  and "che si possa tener e difendere per probabile un'opinione dopo esser
  stata dichiarata e diffinita per contraria alla Sacra Scrittura".
- **The Holy Office, 16 August 1820**: "no obstacles exist for those who
  sustain Copernicus' affirmation regarding the earth's movement in the
  manner in which it is affirmed today" (Brandmüller and Greipl, 1992);
  the imprimatur to Settele in 1822 (dates disputed, Fantoli, Coyne); the
  Index without Copernicus and Galileo, 1835.
- **John Paul II**, address of 31 October 1992: "The majority of
  theologians did not recognize the formal distinction between Sacred
  Scripture and its interpretation, and this led them unduly to transpose
  into the realm of the doctrine of the faith a question which in fact
  pertained to scientific investigation"; Baronius, "Spiritui Sancto mentem
  fuisse nos docere quomodo ad coelum eatur, non quomodo coelum
  gradiatur". Galileo, *Letter to the Grand Duchess Christina* (1615):
  "the mobility or stability of the earth or sun is neither a matter of
  faith nor one contrary to ethics".
- **The findings**: Bradley's aberration of starlight (*Phil. Trans.* 406,
  1729), "the first direct observational evidence of the annual revolution
  of the Earth"; Bessel's parallax of 61 Cygni (1838); Foucault's pendulum
  (1851), "clear dynamical proof of Earth's rotation".
- **Lateran IV** (DH 800): God "simul ab initio temporis utramque de nihilo
  condidit creaturam".
- **Pius XII, "Un'ora"** (22 November 1951; Papal Addresses to the PAS):
  science has indicated the cosmos's "beginning in time at a period about
  five billion years ago, confirming with the concreteness of physical
  proofs the contingency of the universe and the well-founded deduction
  that about that time the cosmos issued from the hand of the Creator.
  Creation, therefore, in time, and therefore, a Creator; and
  consequently, God!"; yet "the facts verified up to now are not
  arguments of absolute proof of creation in time". Lemaître objected to
  "the confusion made between Beginning and Creation"; the address of 1952
  does not repeat it (UCLouvain Archives; Tanzella-Nitti).
- **The age of the universe**: about 13.8 billion years (Planck 2018
  results VI, doi 10.1051/0004-6361/201833910; the figure read at
  encoding).
- **Vienne** (1312, DH 902): "quod anima rationalis seu intellectiva non sit
  forma corporis humani per se et essentialiter, tamquam haereticus sit
  censendus". **Lateran V** (DH 1440): the soul "pro corporum, quibus
  infunditur, multitudine singulariter multiplicabilis et multiplicata et
  multiplicanda".
- ***Humani generis*** 36 (DH 3896): "animas enim a Deo immediate creari
  catholica fides nos retinere iubet"; CCC 366. Thomas, ST I q. 118 a. 2:
  the intellectual soul "cannot be caused through generation, but only
  through creation by God".
- ***Donum vitae*** I.1 (1987): "no experimental datum can be in itself
  sufficient to bring us to the recognition of a spiritual soul"; "how
  could a human individual not be a human person? The Magisterium has not
  expressly committed itself to an affirmation of a philosophical nature";
  "The human being is to be respected and treated as a person from the
  moment of conception" (CCC 2274). *Dignitas personae* 5;
  *Evangelium vitae* 60: "the mere probability that a human person is
  involved would suffice to justify an absolutely clear prohibition".
- **Twinning**: a monozygotic embryo splitting before about day 13 gives
  twins; later, conjoined twins (StatPearls, "Twin Births" and "Conjoined
  Twins"; ISUOG). Forms: parapagus dicephalus, two heads on one trunk;
  craniopagus, joined at the cranium.
- **One will, one death, one baptism, one bond.** Constantinople III
  (DH 556): wills follow natures; Heb 9:27 and DH 1000; Trent, sess. VII,
  can. 9 (DH 1609), and c. 845 §1: baptism "cannot be repeated"; c. 1085
  §1: one "bound by the bond of a prior marriage ... invalidly attempts
  marriage".
- **Thomas on the soul divided**: ST I q. 76 a. 2, "the multiplicity of
  souls is in proportion to the multiplicity of the bodies"; annulose
  animals have "one soul actually and many potentially" (*Summa contra
  Gentiles* II c. 86; not ST I q. 76 a. 8, as the roadmap had it).
- **CIC 1917 c. 748**: "Monstra et ostenta semper baptizentur saltem sub
  conditione; in dubio autem unusne an plures sint homines, unus absolute
  baptizetur, ceteri sub conditione." The Code of 1983 has no such canon;
  c. 869 §1 treats a doubt whether one was baptized.

## THE LAYER

Fixed here.

- **The empirical kind.** `question_kind(Q,empirical)`: a question about
  what the sciences can observe or measure. No tier settles it: on an
  empirical question no source deletes a branch and none cleaves one, and
  no source weighs. A magisterial or juridical act (tiers 1 to 3) that
  supports a value on it is refused (`act_answers_a_fact`), and so is a
  censure of heresy laid on one (`fact_censured_as_heresy`). Theologians
  (tiers 4 and 5) may hold opinions on it; they weigh nothing.
- **A finding settles it.** `finding(F,Q,V,Year)`: from its year the value
  is fixed, a later finding on the same question superseding an earlier.
  A reading of Scripture that reads a value on the question is then on no
  branch, by inerrancy as it stands (DV 11; *Providentissimus Deus* 18).
- **A finding settles no doctrine.** A finding laid on a doctrinal question
  is refused (`finding_on_a_doctrine`) and changes nothing; a teaching act
  that rests a doctrinal value on a finding is refused
  (`doctrine_rests_on_finding`), the doctrine's value unchanged. Creation
  from nothing concerns being, not a date (DH 800): the age of the world
  answers no doctrinal question.
- **The Fathers' consent, outside faith and morals.** A consent claimed on a
  text not of faith or morals fixes nothing (DH 1507's "in rebus fidei et
  morum"), and is reported so with its claimant
  (`consent_outside_faith_and_morals/2`, `consent_claimant/2`).
- **An act's form disputed.** The Index decree of 1616 is read two ways:
  disciplinary, answering only whether Copernicus's book may be read; or
  doctrinal, answering the earth's motion too. Computed both ways.
- **Method never answers being.** A finding reached by division supports
  claims about the parts. A claim that a whole is "nothing but" its parts,
  resting on such a finding, is refused (`nothing_but_from_division`). For a
  human being it is refused as well by the soul's being the form of the
  body (Vienne, DH 902): the unity of the human being is defined; the unity
  of other organisms is a school's.
- **How many human beings: a fact about souls.** Never computed (rule 3),
  never derived from an outward fact: it enters as a disputed fact, both
  ways. A monozygotic twinning is read four ways (`twin_reading/2`): the
  first continues and a second begins (budding); the first ceases and two
  begin (fission); no person before the split (delayed hominization, a
  school's); one soul, its body two organisms apart in space. Refused on
  every reading: one soul divided (the soul is simple; DH 1440), and a
  second soul from anything but God (*Humani generis* 36; A1). The fourth
  reading meets four outward tests and the Church's practice: two contrary
  acts at once (one soul, one will), one twin dead and the other alive (a
  person dies once, Heb 9:27), each baptized (baptism not repeated,
  DH 1609), each married (c. 1085). On every reading the embryo is to be
  treated as a person (*Donum vitae* I.1): reported, not computed.
- **Conjoined twins: the law computed both ways.** CIC 1917 c. 748, in
  force from 19 May 1918 to 26 November 1983: in doubt whether one or
  several, one baptized absolutely and the others conditionally. After
  1983 no canon answers, and the number stays a disputed fact.
- **A new library**, `empirica.lp`: the empirical kind, findings, the
  consent outside faith and morals, method and being, how many human
  beings. In `icon_authority.lp`: no deletion, cleaving or weight on an
  empirical question. In `canon_law.lp`: CIC 1917 c. 748.

## METHOD

Write `empirica.lp`. In `icon_authority.lp`: the empirical kind kept out of
deletion, cleaving and weight. In `canon_law.lp`: c. 748 of 1917 under its
`@norm`, with the Code of 1917's texts. Data in `icon_history.lp`, as parts
loaded beside the records they speak to: Galileo (Josh 10:12–13 read two
ways, the consent claimed, the censure, the Index decree read two ways, the
sentence of 1633, the act of 1820, the findings); the beginning (Lateran
IV, Pius XII's address, the expansion and the age); method and being (with
`world`); twinning, its tests and its candidates. Cases, all HYPOTHETICAL
where not historical: an act at tier 1 on an empirical question; a finding
on a doctrinal question; "nothing but" claims about a human being and a
lion; a twinning with the four tests; a soul divided; a twin's soul from
the other; conjoined twins born in 1950 and in 2000. Run the whole suite,
the runtimes' boots and the coverage index.

## PREDICTIONS

### EM: the empirical kind

- **EM1.** An empirical question with an act at tier 1 for one value
  (HYPOTHETICAL): both branches stand, nothing cleaves, no weight; the act
  refused (`act_answers_a_fact`).
- **EM2.** A finding fixes the empirical question from its year, not
  before; a later finding supersedes an earlier.
- **EM3.** A finding laid on a doctrinal question: refused
  (`finding_on_a_doctrine`), the doctrine's branches unchanged.

### GL: Galileo

- **GL1.** As of 1616: the earth's motion open, both ways, no preference.
  Josh 10:12–13 read two ways (by appearances; as astronomy). The Fathers'
  consent claimed for the astronomical reading fixes nothing, reported as
  outside faith and morals with its claimant (the censure of 1616). The
  censure refused (`fact_censured_as_heresy`). The Index decree read as
  doctrinal: refused (`act_answers_a_fact`); read as disciplinary: not.
- **GL2.** As of 1633: the sentence's word that the opinion had been
  "dichiarata e diffinita" contrary to Scripture is a claim of a lower tier
  about a higher, and treated as fixing: refused (`self_elevation`).
- **GL3.** As of 1730: the earth's motion settled by Bradley's finding; the
  astronomical reading of Josh 10:12–13 on no branch.
- **GL4.** Whether Copernicus's book may be read: governance. As of 1700,
  no (the Index decree on either reading), "yes" a live shadow; as of 1830,
  yes (the act of 1820, the later act), "no" a live shadow.

### CB: the beginning

- **CB1.** The world's beginning (DH 800) settled at tier 1 as of today.
  Pius XII's argument from the expansion: refused
  (`doctrine_rests_on_finding`); the question's classification unchanged.
- **CB2.** The age of the universe: settled by the finding of 2018 as of
  today, open as of 2000; it answers no doctrinal question, and no
  refusal follows from it.

### MB: method and being

- **MB1.** "A human being is nothing but cells", resting on a finding by
  division: refused twice (`nothing_but_from_division`, and against the
  soul as form, DH 902). "A lion is nothing but cells": refused once, by
  method alone. A claim about the parts, resting on the same finding:
  reported, not refused.

### TW: how many human beings

- **TW1.** A twinning: four branches, one per reading; the number of human
  beings after it reported on each (two, two, two, one), never derived; the
  embryo treated as a person on every branch.
- **TW2.** The four tests: the one-soul reading refused four ways
  (contrary acts at once, a death and a life, two baptisms, two bonds); the
  other readings untouched.
- **TW3.** Refused on every reading: one soul divided
  (`soul_divided`); a twin's soul given by the other twin
  (`being_from_non_god`, as it stands).
- **TW4.** Conjoined twins born in 1950 with the doubt recorded: one
  baptized absolutely, the other conditionally (c. 748 of 1917). Born in
  2000: no canon answers; the number computed both ways.

### M: meta

- **M1.** No earlier output changes: every scenario's atoms compared
  before and after. The TTC-SCHOOLS tables do not change.
- **M2.** Every new rule has a test that fails without it; CL1 and PR4
  stay clean.
- **M3.** No unit of the coverage index changes status; the rule files cite
  CCC 159, 366 and 2274 (54 paragraphs).

## CAVEATS

A question is empirical by the encoder's typing, as a question is doctrinal
by it. Which questions belong to the sciences is not computed; the earth's
motion, the expansion and the age are entered so. A finding is entered with
its year and is not weighed; its method is recorded only where a claim rests
on it. Findings fix their question from their year: a finding later
overturned is superseded by the later one, and the record keeps both.

The 1616 censure's signatories and the 1820 and 1822 dates are as the
secondary literature gives them; the decree of 1822 was not read. The Index
decree's form is a disputed reading of the act, entered as such, not a
finding about the congregation's competence. The sentence of 1633 judged a
person; nothing about him is computed (rule 3), and only its word about the
earlier act is entered.

Pius XII's address hedges ("not arguments of absolute proof"); the refusal
is of the argument's form, a doctrine rested on a finding, and changes no
tier.

c. 748 of 1917 was read in two transcriptions that agree (mercaba.org,
codex-iuris-canonici.de); iuscangreg was not reachable from here and is
cited as the text's home, to be checked against it.

Not encoded, waiting: the readings of quantum mechanics as runtimes; one
intellect and two brains; whose matter a shared organ is; the generative
power and marriage in conjoined twins; the ordinary universal magisterium
as a claim about witnesses (its window, threshold, mode and evidence);
"nothing but" in the other direction; a body of evidence counted by
independence. The consent of the Fathers keeps no conditions; its claimant
is recorded where given, and required nowhere yet.
