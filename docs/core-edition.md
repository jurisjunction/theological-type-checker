# TTC-CORE(5)

## NAME

**ttc-core** — the Grammar of the Icon: signature, axioms, theorems, tests

## DESCRIPTION

This is the formal statement. It condenses an earlier, longer paper, *The
Grammar of the Icon: A Programmatic Statement*, which is not included
here; "the long version" below means that paper.

The theory makes three claims. One typed relational structure underlies
the doctrine of God, theological anthropology and the marriage canons. It
forbids positions people actually hold. And it derives juridical results it
was not built to fit.

It is judged as a research programme (Lakatos): a hit counts as novel only
if it was derived before its target was consulted. It is argument from
fittingness, submitted to the judgment of the Church.

On prior work: the gift-and-acceptance reading of consent and bond belongs
to the Navarra school (Hervada, Viladrich, Franceschi) and to Burke. A gift
rationale for c. 1084 appears in Burke and in the Rota (c. Defilippi,
2003), and a trinitarian ground for the bond in Viladrich, Pastwa and
Ouellet. What is claimed here is narrower: the pieces stated as one typed
theory with a checker.

## SIGNATURE

**Sorts:**
- God (the one source *ad extra*, not a level)
- divine persons
- created persons
- other creatures, by level
- principles: single beings, or contracted pairs \[x, y\]

| Relation | Reads | Carries the grain | Encoded |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| E(g, x) | g gives being to x | yes | `being/2` |
| O(p, x) | p originates x | yes | `orig/2` |
| D(x, y) | x acts on y | no | `acton/2` |
| N(x, y) | x gives itself to y face to face | only through finite totality (A7) | `nupt/2` |
| S(x, y, t) | x, y ordered together to t | no | `side_by_side/3` |

Poles are positions in a relation: Rec_R(x, y) iff R(y, x). There is no
unary pole predicate.

## AUTHORITY

**Rules:**

1. A tier-1 truth source deletes contrary branches.
2. Tier 2 cleaves into a counterfactual shadow if its force is truth,
   otherwise into a live shadow.
3. Tier 3 and juridical acts cleave into live shadows, which name the
   sources whose relaxation would restore them.
4. Non-cleaving sources weigh, tier 3 first.
5. Protection blocks cleaving at or below its tier.
6. The force of tier 2 is fixed only by a strictly higher, non-interpretive
   source; anything else is self-elevation.
7. A non-default value needs a source in force (c. 1058).
8. A papal act's tier follows its form.
9. Governance never answers teaching.

| ID | Case | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| B1–B5 | Immaculate Conception, 1400–1860 | weight favours "no" until 1661; protection keeps "yes" open; 1854 deletes "no" |
| B6 | *De auxiliis* | two schools, no preference |

| B9–B14 | Tier 2's force; the form rule | assumption; self-elevation flagged; form mismatches flagged |
| B15 | Law binds to teaching by reference | owed assent per tier |
| B16, B17 | Tier 3 against tier 2; interpretation as tie-breaker | the higher holds; the interpreted reading preferred |

## CASES

### Infants who die without baptism (L1–L4)

The universal A9 silently excluded limbo, which *Auctorem fidei* protects.
Read as MD 25 words it, A9 is neutral; CCC 1261 tips the preference toward
hope. Refusal (Jn 1:11) became representable.

### Death penalty (DP1–DP7)

The dispute reduces to two typings: the modal type of *Dignitas infinita*,
and the tier of DH 795. Read circumstantially, the 2018 text is consistent
with all prior teaching; read intrinsically, it is an equal-tier tension.

### Remarriage (DR1–DR7, DR3b)

Indissolubility, c. 915 and Trent DH 1661 are settled in every branch. The
dispute is one typing. As governance, *Amoris laetitia* governs. As
teaching, the tension is open, and repetition (SC 29) favours FC 84.

### Analogy (AN1–AN12, UN1–UN3)

| Bridge | God | Creatures |
| --- | --- | --- |
| creation | unreceived | received |
| origination | eternal procession | temporal generation |
| principle | identity of power | closed circuit |
| grain | necessity | norm |

Deny by default (AN4). Projection flagged (AN5). Unbridged crossings
flagged, at run time and statically (AN6–AN8). Lateran IV as a two-sided
test: the family keeps persons and loses processions (AN9); the mind keeps
processions and loses persons (AN10); a complete map is a violation (AN11).
Univocity of mode costs an impossible child or a norm on God (UN1, UN2).

### Communion; extra ecclesiam; the 1962 Missal (EC1–EC10, LT1–LT8)

- **Communion** is a lattice, not a scale. DI 17 ranks communities that
  UR 3 leaves incomparable.
- **Membership** is binary; communion is graded. Florence's "outside" read
  as having no bond: consistent. Read as non-membership: Feeney.
- **The Missal**: the later legislation governs each time (c. 20).
  *Quo primum* cannot bind a successor. A total ban would be governance
  answering a question put in teaching form.

### Tier 3 is not flat (G1–G4, CU1–CU8, SA1–SA2)

The structures are legislator rank (c. 135 §2), document character (LG 25),
custom (cc. 23–28) and salience (warnings only). Interviews weigh nothing.
One earlier result moved (DR3b).

### Schism and the SSPX (SX1–SX23, PR1–PR4)

Classification belongs to the authority layer. Incurring, declaration and
validity belong to the canon layer, as yes/no rules. On the public record:

- six bishops presumed incurred (c. 1387)
- the declaration suspended by the recourse (c. 1353)
- the 2016 grant in force; absolution valid, and licit on a just-cause
  request (c. 1335 §2)
- marriages without delegation invalid
- c. 1717 §1 dispenses the investigation, not the hearing

The law follows the theological branch: where the 2026 act is not
classified as schismatic, c. 1364 does not apply. See
[cases/sspx-2026.md](cases/sspx-2026.md).

## DIAGNOSTICS

Every rule, when deleted, turns some test red. If one does not, the test is
missing, not the rule.

## REFUTATION

The programme fails on any of these:

1. **Dogmatic collision**: a defined teaching that no axiom change can
   accommodate without breaking another model.
2. **Terms breakdown**: contradictory verdicts on fully typed relations.
3. **Retrofit exposure**: a retrodiction that fails once its target is
   removed from the warrants.
4. **Degenerating shift**: an axiom added only to silence a test.
5. **The Joachim event**: the framework used to schedule history.

The reservation of orders enters as given (OS 4), with the nuptial sign as
fittingness (*Inter insigniores* 5).

## BUGS

to be". X5 forbade ranking states of life, which Trent does (DH 1810); it

considered. There are probably more.
